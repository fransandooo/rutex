\chapter{Planning}
\label{chap:planning}

Theses need schedules. \Cref{fig:gantt} is a Gantt chart drawn with
\texttt{pgfgantt}.

\begin{figure}[htbp]
  \centering
  \begin{ganttchart}[
      hgrid, vgrid, x unit=0.55cm, y unit chart=0.6cm,
      title label font=\small, bar label font=\small,
      bar/.append style={fill=RoyalBlue!50},
      milestone/.append style={fill=BrickRed}]{1}{20}
    \gantttitle{Weeks}{20} \\
    \gantttitlelist{1,...,20}{1} \\
    \ganttbar{Literature review}{1}{5} \\
    \ganttbar{Methodology}{4}{9} \\
    \ganttbar{Experiments}{8}{15} \\
    \ganttmilestone{First results}{12} \\
    \ganttbar{Writing}{12}{19} \\
    \ganttmilestone{Submission}{20}
  \end{ganttchart}
  \caption{A thesis plan.}
  \label{fig:gantt}
\end{figure}
