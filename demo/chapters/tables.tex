\chapter{Tables}
\label{chap:tables}

Good tables use few lines. \Cref{tab:results} uses \texttt{booktabs},
\texttt{multirow}, and \texttt{siunitx} columns that align numbers on the
decimal point.

\begin{table}[htbp]
  \centering

    \toprule
    \textbf{Model} & \textbf{Dataset} & {\textbf{Accuracy (\%)}} & {\textbf{Time (s)}} \\
    \midrule
    \multirow{2}{*}{Baseline}    & CIFAR-10 & 81.20 & 120.4 \\
                                 & MNIST    & 97.85 &  35.0 \\
    \addlinespace
    \multirow{2}{*}{Transformer} & CIFAR-10 & 92.47 & 410.9 \\
                                 & MNIST    & 99.31 &  88.2 \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}
